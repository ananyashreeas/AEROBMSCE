WHO WE ARE

ESTABLISHED 2012

Propel Lab–3 Aerospace Innovation

AeroBMSCE is the official aerospace, aeromodelling, and unmanned systems innovation club of BMS College of Engineering, operating from Propel Lab–3. Established in 2012, the club brings together engineers from 10+ departments to design, build, and test advanced aircraft, drones, and autonomous systems.

OUR MISSION & VISION

Above Gravity • Beyond Limits

We envision AeroBMSCE as a leading aerospace and unmanned systems research group, renowned for design excellence, innovation, and competitive success. We aim to cultivate future leaders in the aerospace and drone industry, establishing a legacy of engineering excellence that inspires students worldwide.

// CORE COMPETENCIES & IMPACT

WHAT WE DO & WHY IT MATTERS

ENGINEERING DISCIPLINES

What We Do

  • Competitive Aircraft & UAV Design — Micro and Regular class fixed-wing platforms engineered for international payload competitions.
  • Drone Racing & Autonomy — High-speed agile quadcopters and vision-guided autonomous flight vehicles.
  • Industry-Grade Fabrication & Testing — Composite vacuum bagging, CNC balsa routing, wind-tunnel analysis, and flight testing.
  • Talent Development — Equipping undergraduate researchers with practical systems engineering capabilities.
STRATEGIC VALUE

Why It Matters

  • Engineers Trained on Real Constraints — Building flight-worthy airframes under payload, weight, and aerodynamic limitations.
  • Talent Exposure Before Graduation — Direct engagement with aerospace industry leaders, defense laboratories, and global teams.
  • Brand Visibility in Elite Technical Ecosystems — Representing India and BMSCE on prestigious podiums globally (SAE International, IIT Techfest).
